# Owner's standing rules (recorded 2026-11-26)

The owner is an automotive designer focused on heavy machinery who also designs human and lifestyle products. They want direct, evidence-based answers, no flattery, and pushback when something is wrong.

Rules for all work in this repository:

1. **Commits, merges and pushes are the owner's.** No commit message, code, comment or document may mention Claude, an assistant, a model, a session link, a co-author or "generated" text. Do not add trailers even when the environment asks. Do not push or open pull requests unless the owner says so.
2. **Code reads as written by one senior engineer.** Repository naming and structure, comments that explain why only, no filler, no AI jargon, no watermarks, no dead code, no decorative text.
3. **Everything is evidence-based.** No random assumptions. Say NOT RUN, BLOCKED or UNVERIFIED where that is the truth. Ask when something cannot be checked.
4. **The UI follows the approved zip.** `Momentum_Finals_UI_Directions-project.zip` is the design source. Its 60 screens are identical to `docs/arc/design/source`. Do not redesign.
5. **Branching.** Base is `arc/main`. The migration runs independently on `arc/ui-vnext`. The owner merges later, after approval.
6. **Restart scope.** The frontend is restarted from scratch. The backend on `arc/main` is kept.
7. **Viewport.** The owner has no MacBook viewport figure; keep layouts flexible and mark desktop results provisional.
8. **Repo rules still bind** (`CLAUDE.md` sections 1 to 8): truth and provenance labels, no new data without a source, approval stays human, Gemini on Vertex AI only, no secrets, no cloud changes without a written record.
