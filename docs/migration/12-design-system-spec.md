# 12 Design system spec (extracted)

Source of truth: `docs/arc/design/source/README.md` and the 60 HTML files. Tokens in `web/public/arc-tokens.css` were verified by the repo's own QA (ARC-M6-01: 41 of 41 names, 102 selector/name/value pairs). This audit did not re-diff them.

## Layout
- Design width 1440. Sidebar 232. Top bar 56. Content padding 24. 12 columns of 82 with 16 gaps (1160 content width).
- Radii: card 12, button and input 10, chip 6. Nav item height 44, icon 20, label 16/500.
- Arc opens as a right-hand workspace over the dimmed page; full-width sheet on phones.
- Kraft emphasis surface `.kr`: one per page, for the most important card (values in the design README).

## Type
- Inter for text, Inter Tight for headings and numbers (`.d`, `.n`, tabular figures). Key values at least 24px, primary actions at least 16px. Base 15px/1.4.
- Fonts: the HTML loads both from Google Fonts. `web/` self-hosts Inter (with arrow glyphs U+2190 to U+2193 added). Inter Tight local files: NOT VERIFIED (DR-032).

## Not specified by the design (do not invent; PD-04)
Hover, focus, pressed, disabled styling beyond what is frozen in HTML; motion and transition timings; z-index scale; breakpoints other than 1440 and phone; chart styling rules beyond the two chart screens; loading skeletons; the 17 gaps G1 to G17.
