Production Preflight

Challenge the plan before production does.

Research directionRequires validated data
Problem
A plan can pass at one point and fail nearby.
How it works
Vary conditions, re-run the limits, report weak spots.
Needs
Validated models and approved ranges.
Human step
A supervisor reads the report first.
Limit
Passing a simulation is not physical proof.
Today
New Lot compares settings once, at entered conditions.

Experiment Designer

Design the smallest responsible trial to learn.

Research direction
Problem
Some settings stay tied because data cannot separate them.
How it works
Propose a small trial with controls and stop rules.
Needs
Measuring tools, an inspection protocol, approval.
Human step
Safety and operations review before any trial.
Limit
It never changes machine settings on its own.
Today
No trials are designed or run.

Proof-Carrying Job Cards

Every instruction carries its evidence.

Research direction
Problem
A job card shows settings, not why they were chosen.
How it works
Attach sources, limits and approval to a stored record.
Needs
Hashed revisions and verified approver identity.
Human step
The approver signs the exact revision.
Limit
A checksum is not a compliance certificate.
Today
The approved PDF is stored and read back.

Factory Time Machine

Replay what was known at the time.

Requires validated data
Problem
Past decisions get judged with today's hindsight.
How it works
Rebuild the old inputs, limits and model version.
Needs
Saved snapshots, model versions, real outcomes.
Human step
A reviewer compares the replay with what happened.
Limit
A replay is modeled, never a measured saving.
Today
History is modeled, not measured.

Production Fingerprints

Recognize a line's usual operating signature.

Requires validated data
Problem
New lots can differ in ways nobody checks.
How it works
Learn usual ranges, flag unfamiliar combinations.
Needs
Validated records per machine and material.
Human step
A supervisor decides what to inspect.
Limit
Unusual is not the same as defective.
Today
No measured per-machine history exists.

Inverse Production Planning

Start with the goal, not the settings.

Research direction
Problem
Supervisors think in goals, not seven settings.
How it works
Set goals. A solver lists feasible plans within limits.
Needs
Approved ranges and tested objectives.
Human step
A supervisor reviews the chosen plan.
Limit
Gemini never invents settings.
Today
The solver tries 8,640 options, three changes at most.

Closed-Loop Quality Learning

Compare what was predicted with what ran.

Research directionRequires validated data
Problem
Predictions are never checked against real lots.
How it works
Record outcomes, measure drift, review model updates.
Needs
Measured scrap, QC results, a model release gate.
Human step
People approve every model update.
Limit
Samples alone cannot prove savings.
Today
Four of five models miss the holdout bar.

Factory Decision Memory

Keep why a decision was made, and what followed.

Requires validated data
Problem
Teams keep the setting and lose the reason.
How it works
Find similar past decisions and what happened next.
Needs
Recorded rationale, outcomes, access rules.
Human step
A supervisor reviews any precedent before use.
Limit
Similar is not causal. Rules are never copied.
Today
Each lot keeps its own approval record only.
