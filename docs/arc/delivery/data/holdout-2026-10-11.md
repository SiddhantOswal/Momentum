# Fresh generated holdout, October and November 2026 (ARC-ML-02)

Run 2026-11-25 by Om's session. Label: **generated lots, modelled on the plant's real item master; not plant measurements.**

## Why
September 2026 informed the D4-2026-11-20-OM decision to switch to the `m2_*` models, so it cannot also be the test that confirms them. This holdout played no part in training or choosing either model set: `m2_*` trained on lots before 1 July 2026, `m_*` on lots up to 31 August 2026.

## What this supports and what it does not
- Supports: the `m2_*` models generalise to a later period of the same modelled plant, and the September claim "all five score higher" holds on fresh lots for R², while only Print (and Warp on squared error) is a clear paired improvement.
- Does not support: any claim about real plant accuracy or measured savings. The lots come from the same generator the models were trained on. A plant-measured holdout (audit M06, evidence request to Om) is still needed.
- The bootstrap resamples lots, not orders or reels; the generated files carry no group field.
