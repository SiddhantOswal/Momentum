Momentum: pre-production checks for manufacturing plants

Pre-production checks for manufacturing plants. Every job read, risk-scored and approved before it reaches the line.

Scroll

The expensive mistakes in a plant are made before the machine starts.

A job arrives. Someone reads the spec, someone recalls the settings that worked last time, and the run begins. When the output warps or the finish drifts, the waste is already on the pallet.

Momentum sits in front of that moment. It reads the job, scores the defect risk, proposes settings from your own history and asks a supervisor to approve before anything is cut.

Seven steps from purchase order to job card.

Each step leaves a record, and the approval is logged with the job card.

  1. 01

    Upload the order

    Drop in the purchase order. Gemini reads the document and pulls out the spec.

  2. 02

    Validate the spec

    Extracted values are checked against what the plant can actually make.

  3. 03

    Confirm the conditions

    The operator confirms board, tooling, moisture and the line slot.

  4. 04

    Predict the risk

    A model estimates the risk of warping, delamination, strength, print quality and cracking.

  5. 05

    Optimise the settings

    A search over the allowed setting ranges finds settings that respect every limit and lower the predicted risk.

  6. 06

    Show the evidence

    Each recommendation points back to the past runs it came from.

  7. 07

    Approve and release

    A supervisor approves. The job card is issued with the settings attached.
